RESEARCH FOCUS  (dissertation / current work)
==========================================================================
Thesis: "Human–AI Co-Intelligence in VR for Design Comprehension: Effects of a
Conversational AI Agent's Embodiment and Dialogue Style."

Plain-language summary:
Amir builds VR co-design environments where an AI co-designer supports hybrid
human–AI thinking, and runs experimental user studies on how the agent's embodiment
(a body or just a voice) and dialogue style (concise vs. exploratory, supportive vs.
challenging, etc.) shape spatial reasoning, decision-making, and learning during
creative tasks. The aim is reliable human-centered AI support — productive cognitive
extension without overreliance or overtrust.

Tech he uses for this: Unity (C#), modern LLM/VLM pipelines including RAG and fine-tuning
(LoRA / QLoRA), tool-calling agentic patterns, and quantitative+qualitative user-study
methods.

SELECTED PROJECTS  (link to the case-study page when asked)
==========================================================================

THESIS PROJECT — Human–AI Co-Intelligence in VR for Design Comprehension
   The dissertation work itself. Investigates how a conversational AI agent's
   embodiment and dialogue style affect design reasoning, comprehension, and decisions.
   Builds Unity-based VR co-design + learning environments. Translates 3D scene state
   and user actions into structured contextual inputs for the AI, so the agent's
   dialogue is grounded in what the user is currently looking at. Evaluated through
   experimental user studies. (No standalone case-study page yet — discussed across
   the site's Home and Activities pages.)

STANDARDSCOUT — RAG-Based Building Code Assistant     /projects/standardscout.html
   LLM-powered building-code copilot. Local RAG over ADA, ABA, and GSA P-100, plus
   optional session-only PDF upload and optional .gov web search. Fine-tuned Llama 3.1
   8B Instruct for friendly, structured, citation-grounded answers, with a fine-tuned
   GPT-4.1 mini as an alternative. First-layer agent classifies user intent, maintains
   conversation context, refines queries, and routes to the appropriate retrieval path.
   Generates a blueprint-style technical diagram per Q&A. Two UI modes (Text and
   Conversational) share identical retrieval logic — only the UI differs.
   Live demo: embedded on /standardscout.html and on the case study page.
   Hugging Face Space: https://huggingface.co/spaces/Amir-XR/StandardScout

ARTH — Unity VR Design and Learning Environments for Adaptive Reuse  /projects/project-1.html
   Unity (C#) VR learning environments for adaptive reuse design tasks. Tablet-driven
   placement, demolition, and editing modes plus study instrumentation. Designed to
   support controlled studies of AI-assisted design workflows, including future
   manipulations of agent embodiment and dialogue style.

GAODE — Multimodal Architectural Design Serious Game      /projects/project-2.html
   Open-ended educational serious game for early architectural design learning inside
   a parametric CAD workflow (Rhino/Grasshopper). Multimodal NUI: Leap Motion hand
   gestures, machine-vision finger-count actions (webcam + OpenCV), and offline keyword
   speech commands — keyboard/mouse free but intentional and low-error. Five
   "iconic-building" game modes target different design-cognition skills.
   Published as a peer-reviewed paper (Education and Information Technologies, 2022).

BESOLOGY — Parametric Topology Optimization              /projects/project-3.html
   Performance-driven workflow combining parametric modeling with BESO topology
   optimization for high-rise structural design under wind and gravity loads.
   Published in Journal of Architectural Engineering, 2021.

OROSI — Interactive Pavilion                              /projects/project-4.html
   Responsive light + kinetic installation inspired by Iranian Orosi windows.
   Computational design + interaction.

FABRICATION PROJECTS                                      /projects/project-5.html
   Selected digital fabrication work — robotic workflows, CNC + laser, full-scale
   prototypes.

HUMAN BRAIN CUTTING MATRIX  (KU Medical Center, patent submitted)
   Interdisciplinary collaboration with the KU Medical Center to build a Human Brain
   Cutting Matrix using digital fabrication and 3D scanning. Translated clinical/lab
   requirements into manufacturable, replicable designs. Advanced through the U.S.
   patenting process via the KU Center for Technology Commercialization.
   Documented as an abstract in /publications.html (Posters and abstracts).
