CURRENT FOCUS
==========================================================================
Amir builds AI systems for complex architectural and engineering information:
drawings, technical documents, and project records. His focus is on connecting
information to inspectable evidence, useful interfaces, and human review.

Current research in progress: adaptive evidence investigation for architectural
records. This explores how AI can establish what a source supports for a particular
question, inspect qualifications, revise interpretations, and identify unresolved
questions. The approach integrates multimodal retrieval, provenance-aware knowledge
graphs, and tool-mediated reasoning. It is ongoing research; comparative results
and improved accuracy have not been established here.

His earlier work in computational design, digital fabrication, VR, and human-computer
interaction provides the foundation for this direction. The earlier VR co-intelligence
proposal is no longer his current doctoral research.

ADAPTIVE EVIDENCE INVESTIGATION — Research in progress   /#research
   Investigates how AI can connect architectural records to claim-specific evidence
   judgments and targeted source inspection. A matching detail may apply to a
   different building zone; the system must investigate that qualification before
   using it to support a conclusion. A short overview is on the homepage; describe
   this as ongoing research, not completed findings or an available product.

HAD — Human–AI Design (working name in the prototype: HAT)   /projects/had.html
   Independent prototype (2025) exploring the human–AI design process: a person
   sketches a plan and designs it in conversation with an AI assistant that sees the
   plan, asks what they are designing and which room to focus on, talks about the
   plan, and acts on it through tools. Like a surgical assistant handing over
   instruments, it can activate the wall tool for the person (solid 20 cm or interior
   10 cm) and say where to start and end, hand them a door, window or item that
   snaps into place on their click, or act itself: place furniture through the solver,
   move, rotate, swap or remove objects, all undoable. It
   is not a furniture-placement app; placing a bed is the worked example of the
   collaboration. When the person asks for
   something, the model turns the request into a typed placement instruction with no
   coordinates (item, room, anchor such as the longest
   wall or a corner, facing, a reference to another piece), and a deterministic solver
   places it on a structured design model and checks it (inside the room, no wall or
   furniture overlap, clear of the door swing, window view kept) before committing.
   The design model derives rooms from the wall network (endpoint clustering,
   T-junction splitting, face tracing) and keeps doors and windows parametric on
   their walls. Task-specific scene context: a spoken room summary for conversation,
   a planning survey in 10 cm cells with free-space envelopes for layout planning,
   and a precise brief for relational moves. AI placements commit as undoable
   batches with provenance; walls, doors and windows stay click-to-place. One
   document drives the 2D SVG editor, a three.js view you can walk or fly through on
   screen, and a WebXR walkthrough on a headset (no in-headset editing). Stack: TypeScript/React 19, Zustand, Zod,
   three.js / React Three Fiber, @react-three/xr, OpenAI Realtime over WebRTC
   (gpt-realtime-2), GPT-5-class and Claude models for layout planning,
   claude-haiku-4-5 for relational moves, Cloudflare Workers, Supabase with RLS,
   Vitest (600+ tests). Sole designer and developer. No public repository or demo
   link on the page; a demo video is planned.

STANDARDSCOUT — RAG-Based Building Code Assistant     /projects/standardscout.html
   LLM-powered building-code copilot. Local RAG over ADA, ABA, and GSA P-100, plus
   optional session-only PDF upload and optional .gov web search. Fine-tuned Llama 3.1
   8B Instruct for friendly, structured, citation-grounded answers, with a fine-tuned
   GPT-4.1 mini as an alternative. First-layer agent classifies user intent, maintains
   conversation context, refines queries, and routes to the appropriate retrieval path.
   Generates a blueprint-style technical diagram per Q&A. Two UI modes (Text and
   Conversational) share identical retrieval logic — only the UI differs.
   Live demo: embedded on the case study page.
   Hugging Face Space: https://huggingface.co/spaces/Amir-XR/StandardScout

ARTH — Unity VR Design and Learning Environments for Adaptive Reuse  /projects/project-1.html
   Unity (C#) VR learning environments for adaptive reuse design tasks. Tablet-driven
   placement, demolition, and editing modes plus study instrumentation. Designed to
   support controlled studies of AI-assisted design workflows, including future
   manipulations of agent embodiment and dialogue style.

GAODE — Multimodal Architectural Design Serious Game      /projects/project-2.html
   Open-ended educational serious game for early architectural design learning inside
   a parametric CAD workflow (Rhino/Grasshopper). Multimodal NUI: Leap Motion hand
   gestures, machine-vision finger-count actions (webcam + OpenCV), and offline keyword
   speech commands — keyboard/mouse free but intentional and low-error. Five
   "iconic-building" game modes target different design-cognition skills.
   Published as a peer-reviewed paper (Education and Information Technologies, 2022).

BESOLOGY — Parametric Topology Optimization              /projects/project-3.html
   Performance-driven workflow combining parametric modeling with BESO topology
   optimization for high-rise structural design under wind and gravity loads.
   Published in Journal of Architectural Engineering, 2021.

OROSI — Interactive Pavilion                              /projects/project-4.html
